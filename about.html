A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Hi, I'm Claudia.

I'm still discovering which part of cyber security excites me most, and that's what makes being a student so interesting.

My story so far

I'm in my second year of a BSc(Hons) Cyber Security degree at the University of Hertfordshire. I wanted to study something that would challenge me, help me think logically and give me useful skills for a world that depends more and more on technology.

Some topics click immediately. Others take a few attempts, a lot of notes and a little patience! I've learned that solving a difficult problem is one of the most satisfying parts of studying computing.

This website is part of that journey. It started as separate HTML pages in Notepad++ and has gradually become a place where I can show my progress.

Outside the code

I like projects that connect technology to real people. That's one reason I enjoyed our EcoTrace project : it made us think about the wider impact of AI, not just how to build an application.
